# 🌱 Thirukkural Animated Story – முயற்சி தரும் பெருமை

An interactive and animated educational webpage that teaches the Thirukkural:

> **“அருமை உடைத்தென்று அசாவாமை வேண்டும்
> பெருமை முயற்சி தரும்.”**

The project explains the importance of **perseverance, continuous effort, and never giving up** through a simple story that school students can easily understand.

## 📖 About the Project

Instead of presenting the Thirukkural only as text, this project turns its meaning into a **short interactive story**.

The story follows **Kavya**, a young student who wants to become good at drawing. Her first attempt fails, but instead of giving up, she continues practising. Eventually, her effort leads to success.

This helps students understand the message:

> **“Something may be difficult, but we should not give up. Continuous effort leads to success.”**

## 💡 Moral of the Story

The project teaches students that:

**Failure is not the end.**

A seed does not become a tree in one day. It needs time, care and continuous growth.

Similarly, when we face difficulties, we should not give up. By continuing to try, learn and improve, we can achieve success.

## 🌟 Educational Approach

The project uses **storytelling + animation + interaction** instead of traditional text-based teaching.

Students first experience the character's problem, see the consequences of giving up, understand the importance of effort, and then connect the story with the Thirukkural.

This makes the concept easier to remember and understand.
